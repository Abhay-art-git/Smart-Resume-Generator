# 📄 Smart Resume Generator

## 📘 Overview
The **Smart Resume Generator** is an AI-powered web application that allows users to **create professional, well-formatted resumes automatically** by simply providing their details.  
It leverages **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** and **predefined templates** to generate resumes that are **ATS-friendly** (Applicant Tracking System) and visually appealing.  

This tool helps job seekers save time, improve resume quality, and stand out in competitive job markets.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Automate the process of resume creation.  
- Ensure resumes are **professional, readable, and ATS-compatible**.  
- Provide multiple **templates and styling options**.  
- Allow easy **export to PDF** and shareable formats.  

---

## 🧩 Features
- ✅ **User Input Form** – Enter personal, educational, and professional details.  
- ✅ **Multiple Resume Templates** – Choose from different pre-designed layouts.  
- ✅ **AI-Powered Content Enhancement** – Suggests improved phrasing for skills, experience, and achievements.  
- ✅ **Export to PDF** – Download resume in high-quality PDF format.  
- ✅ **Responsive Design** – Works on desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.  
- ✅ **Optional Integration** – Connects with LinkedIn or other profile platforms for auto-import.

## 🧪 Workflow
1. User fills in personal, educational, and professional details.  
2. User selects a **resume template**.  
3. AI engine suggests improvements and formats the content.  
4. Resume is generated and previewed.  
5. User downloads resume as **PDF** or shares it online.
